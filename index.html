About the Academy

Building capability across the Public Service

The Academy for Civil Service Excellence is the central professional development institution for Square Country's civil service.

We provide education, leadership development and professional training for public servants at every stage of their careers. Our programmes support effective administration, ethical decision-making, evidence-based policy and high standards of public service.
